OH-Xchange

Minimizing downtime in PET preform production.

Our OH-Xchange programme offers an exclusive hot runner exchange service for PET preform converters and bottlers – unmatched in the industry.

我们的流程

Streamlining your path to success.

Our expert team works closely with you to understand your specific production requirements. We analyse your needs, recommend the optimal mould configuration and offer continuous support to ensure peak performance. We guide you through every step from initial concept to final implementation.

Issue identification

When a hot runner issue occurs or refurbishment is needed, the customer contacts us.

Rapid replacement

A fully refurbished, like-for-like hot runner is quickly dispatched to the customer.

Minimal downtime

Production typically resumes within two days of starting the work.

Return and refurbishment

The original hot runner is sent back to Otto Hofstetter AG for comprehensive overhaul.

Continuous availability

The refurbished system joins the stock of exchange units, ready for the next customer need.

Benfits

Key benefits of our exclusive exchange programme.

Drastically reduced downtime

Maintained production quality
